*The up and coming snail tag champion*

Jeremiah is a beige snail who is a rookie snail tag competitor. He is most often found on [[Corinthia]]'s hat, moving at a pace that is typical of a snail.

## Appearance

![Jeremiah on a leaf](/images/jeremiah.png)

Jeremiah is an above average size snail who's shell is brown with a spiral pattern and a small crack at the top. His eyestalks are longer than usual, and he leaves a trail of slippery slime.

## Personality

- **Temperament:** Eager to compete
- **Speech:** He doesn't
- **Quirks:** 
  - Drawn to black marble
  - Very overconfident and does not believe that salt is deadly
- **Goals:** 
  - To become the snail tag world champion
  - Give birth
  - To find home
